How FoodFlow Works

A simple, safe, and accountable three-step process built to ensure food reaches people with dignity and zero delay.

Donors Post Surplus Food

Restaurants, grocers, and households post available surplus with portions, description, pickup location, and exact expiry deadline.

Recipients Receive Alerts & Claim

Verified charities, shelters, and families receive local alerts, inspect details, and reserve the exact quantity needed.

Verified Physical Handover

The recipient presents their unique 8-character verification token at pickup. The donor verifies it instantly on FoodFlow to complete handover.

FOR FOOD BUSINESSES & RESTAURANTS

Turn Surplus into Nourishment in Minutes

Donating food shouldn't be complicated. FoodFlow gives your kitchen staff a clean, 2-minute posting interface that schedules pickups before food quality degrades.

1

Snap & Describe Portions

Upload a photo, select dietary tags (halal, vegetarian, dairy-free), and specify the total portions ready for collection.

2

Set Clear Expiry & Pickup Windows

Define when pickup is allowed. Our system auto-calculates urgency badges and disables claiming when the deadline passes.

3

Confirm Handover with 1-Click Code

When the verified recipient arrives, enter their 8-character pickup code to immediately record the completed rescue.
